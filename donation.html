Donate to Edunova Foundation

Edunova Foundation is building a public, education-focused 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Every donation supports scholarships for high school, college, and university students.

Donate / Contact Us How funds are used Gift for $50+

Our Mission

Education as a Public Good

We exist to expand access to quality education—helping students stay motivated, confident, and supported throughout their learning journey.

Scholarships for Students

Every dollar is directed to scholarship programs for high school, college, and university students—especially families facing tuition pressure or low-income challenges.

Volunteer-Run, Mission-Driven

Our team members are volunteers. We do not take salaries from Edunova—so your support stays focused on students and programs.

What Your Donation Supports

Need-Based Scholarships

For families under financial pressure—helping students continue learning without giving up opportunities.

Merit & Motivation Awards

Recognizing effort, responsibility, and academic growth—encouraging teenagers and young adults to keep going.

Education Programs (Low-Cost / Mostly Free)

Most services are free. Some services charge a small fee only to cover operating costs—so the foundation can sustain long-term support.

Transparency commitment: We operate under bylaws and compliance standards. We will continue improving reporting and verification to earn long-term trust.

Thank-You Gift for $50+ Donations

Edunova Logo Gift

For donations of $50 or more, we offer an Edunova logo gift as a small token of appreciation.

  • Gift availability may vary by region and inventory.
  • We will contact you after donation to confirm delivery details.

Prefer to stay anonymous?

That’s totally fine. We respect donor privacy. If you prefer, you can donate anonymously—just let us know.

Tip: If you want a receipt for your records, include your email when you contact us.

How to Donate

We currently accept multiple donation methods. To donate, please contact us—our team will respond with the correct details for your preferred method.

Zelle

Fast and convenient. Contact us to confirm the Zelle recipient details.

Check

We can provide the payee name and mailing instructions.

Other donation options

If you prefer another method, let us know—we’ll do our best to support it.

Contact email: Please reach out to team@edunovafdn.org to donate.
Will donations be used for staff salaries?

No. Our team members are volunteers and do not take salaries from Edunova. Donations are directed to scholarships and program operations.

Do you support low-income families?

Yes. We plan scholarship categories that specifically address tuition pressure and low-income challenges.

How can I confirm legitimacy and transparency?

We operate under organizational bylaws and compliance standards for public, charitable work. We will continue improving reporting to earn long-term trust.
